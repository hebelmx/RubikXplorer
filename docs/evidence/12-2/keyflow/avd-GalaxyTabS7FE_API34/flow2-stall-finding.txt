# Flow 2 stall on GalaxyTabS7FE_API34: diagnosis (2026-11-11, revision 0ec0d6baa77328a108e7cd92cca7778abf3b9656)

Rerun of flow2TwoLookLastLayer alone on the AVD (SwiftShader, headless), with a screenshot and a
uiautomator dump every 3 s after the twoLookLesson surface. Result again:
  flow2: timed out waiting for Move f to commit

App window (uiautomator): [725,0][1835,1600] on a 2560x1600 @ 320 dpi landscape display. The forced
portrait window is letterboxed: 1110 x 1432 px content = 555 x 716 dp (COMPACT).

flow2-stall-uiautomator.xml (after "moveKey:more" was tapped; toggle reads "Hide wide and slice turns"):
  face row     y 498..594 px  (U .. D' keys)
  rotation row y 610..706 px  (Hide toggle, x .. z2)
  wide row     absent (f, f', ... not exposed)
  slice row    absent
flow2-stall-screenshot.png also shows the system letterbox education dialog ("See and do more") over
the app. It is not the cause: the "more" tap under it took effect, and Compose test clicks are
dispatched to the app's view, not through the system window stack.

Robolectric probe at the same window size (qualifiers w555dp-h716dp, AppShellTest launch, White
cross Lesson, tap moveKey:more, then tap moveKey:f; temporary, not committed):
  lesson:stage = top 64 dp, bottom 345 dp
  moveKey:f    = top 336 dp, bottom 384 dp   (48 dp target, but 39 of its 48 dp lie below the stage)
  lesson:panel = top 353 dp, bottom 716 dp
  session revision 1 -> 1 after the tap: the Move did not commit.

Cause: in COMPACT the stage has a fixed height (max(0.45 x the Lesson body height, 280 dp + gutters)) and the
Move key bar sits inside it, under the cube, in no vertical scroll. Expanding "more" adds the wide and
slice rows, which overflow the stage's bottom edge under the panel; the tap at the key's centre lands
on the panel. A Learner in a 555 x 716 dp window cannot press any wide or slice key. App bug, not a
harness bug; not fixed in Story 12.2.
