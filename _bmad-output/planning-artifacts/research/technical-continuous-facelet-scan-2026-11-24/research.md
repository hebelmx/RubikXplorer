# Feasibility of continuous facelet scanning

**Proceed with a PC-first algorithm spike — without Super-Morse's weights — and keep both tablets in the later device trial.** Auditing Super-Morse YOLO11n against Cubentia's own labeled fixtures revealed an upstream synthetic-label bug that breaks its 3-face localization (**29.5% exact cell+color accuracy** on `Fixtures/corner/` vs. **88.9%** on valid single frontal faces). Do not build the multi-face PC replay on it. Instead, prove the continuous mapping, deferred global color clustering, graded observation quality, and regrip-recovery algorithm on PC first — starting with verified keyframes and either read-only PC comparators (`yush-io/cubeSolver` single-class `sticker`, `YORO` 6-color OBB) or a small LiteRT model trained from an extended `tools/corner-model/render_cube.py` — before writing new Android stories or setting a Galaxy S24 hardware floor. [1] [2] [3] [6] [8]

A detector score alone cannot decide that a sticker belongs in a named square. Separating geometry detection from color classification avoids locking the detector to a single lighting/cube palette. All candidate weights, dataset splits, and license limitations are catalogued in the [detector digest](digests/facelets.md).

## What worked on our photos

Scoring Super-Morse `best.onnx` on Cubentia's own labeled photos exposed why proposal counts alone were misleading ([still-probe.md](digests/still-probe.md)):

- **Raw counts hid a device confound:** Across all 46 corner photos, stickered counts were 25–27 on both Lenovo (median 26) and Galaxy S24 FE (median 26.5), while stickerless split sharply by device: **Galaxy S24 FE median 24 (22–27)** vs. **Lenovo median 17 (6–22)** — masked by the combined stickerless median of 19.
- **3-face accuracy collapsed despite high counts:** On all 30 human-keypoint-labeled `Fixtures/corner/` photos (810 ground-truth stickers, pre-framed squares), Super-Morse achieved only **46.3% unique cell hit (375/810)** and **29.5% exact cell+color accuracy (239/810; 63.7% color given hit)**, with **320 duplicate hits (45.8% of all 699 proposals)** and median box aspect ratio **`w/h = 0.30`** (pastel `stlb` subset: **29.6% hit / 18.5% exact**). Side-face recall was lowest where needed most: top `U`/`D` 63.0% hit (41.5% correct), left `F`/`L` 40.0% (25.9%), right `R`/`B` 35.9% (21.1%). `S24 FE / stickered` had 210 proposals yet only **17.6% exact accuracy (38/216)** due to 102 duplicate/shifted hits.
- **Single frontal faces worked cleanly (confirming the root cause):** On 42 valid frontal `Fixtures/live/` faces (378 stickers, excluding `sm-s721u1_stkb_studio-a`'s corrupted truth), the exact same model scored **96.8% unique hit (366/378)** and **88.9% exact cell+color (336/378)** with square boxes (`w/h = 1.01`). On 73 cube-free `128×128` negative crops (`Fixtures/corner/*/keypoints/*.png`), it fired false positives on **14/73 frames (19.2%, 46 boxes)**.
- **Nine third-party three-face photos (`seandavidreed/rubiks_cube_segmentation`):** Reproduced **229 boxes for 243 labeled stickers**, **118 matched centers (48.6%)**, and **104 color+location matches (42.8%)**, invariant to letterbox/stretch preprocessing (118–120/243). The horizontal box clustering observed on `16_jpg` and `0_jpg` (`w/h = 0.29`) matches the exact upstream synthetic generator bug (`offscreenCamera.aspect = 1.0` for rendering vs. viewport `camera` for `calculateYoloLabels()`). [1] [6] [8]

## How the map can grow

Start by marking visible stickers or face quads in the preview. Fit candidate projective 3×3 grids to sticker centers or face boundaries and retain raw CIELAB/linear-RGB samples alongside viewing angle, glare, sharpness, and source frame. **Test an intrinsics-free 2D projective baseline first** (per-face homographies + shared-edge topology + periodic detector refit) before escalating to calibrated 3D `solvePnP`, since budget tablets often lack calibrated intrinsics and Android builds forbid OpenCV (`CameraSizePolicy.kt`). A lone face — especially a solved, mid-solve, or 90°/180°-symmetric face — can have multiple valid rotations; never lock quarter-turn orientation from a single symmetric face's sticker colors alone. Wait for two readable adjacent centers with a shared edge (with logo-tolerant center handling for Rubik's/GAN white centers) or an unbroken tracked overlap to an asymmetric anchor before locking global orientation. Track points between detector frames, periodically re-detect, and suspend map updates during a regrip. [3] [4]

**Decouple color naming and angle quality from frame-1 greediness.** To avoid poisoning axis locking when a scan starts on Yellow/Orange/Blue, crosses warm/cool light (AWB drift), or uses a pastel cube (`stlb`), store raw sticker color samples and solve global 6-cluster color assignment once enough centers/faces are seen. Decouple **topological stitching** (oblique adjacent faces give shared edges and quarter-turn alignment) from **color commitment**, using a **graded per-sticker quality score** (foreshortening up to ~35° when glare-free + low specular clipping + sharpness) so desk-tablet viewing angles and overhead frontal glare do not deadlock commitments.

**Apply immediate local physical invariants with evidence-weighted conflict reopening.** Full-cube legality runs late and accepts other legal scrambles. Under fixed-layer rotation, enforce four immediate local checks: (1) no identical or opposite colors on a shared edge/corner cubie, (2) valid **corner cyclic-order chirality** (the 8 oriented corner triples), (3) per-color count `≤ 9`, and (4) duplicate-free edge and corner cubie multisets. When a conflict occurs (e.g., an early Red↔Orange misread colliding with a later read), compare accumulated quality/frame support and reopen **only the lower-evidence slot(s)** rather than locking in the earlier read or flickering both faces. Validate completed nets against independently recorded truth.

A published system using a robot, a large cube and a depth camera assembled exact nets in five of five indoor sequences and three of five outdoor sequences; both incomplete scans lacked a usable sixth view, motivating a cue for the missing side. A separate handheld RGB project documents five phone recordings and a failure where a stationary-corner rule captured only two faces despite readable colors on all six — confirming that stability must be tested in the moving face's frame. [3] [4]

The first PC spike must run the same mapper on **human-verified keyframes** first, then on an **unpoisoned automatic detector**. If it misplaces verified inputs, fix registration, color clustering, and invariants before touching models or Android.

## Is a Galaxy S24 minimum warranted?

**Keep the Lenovo Tab M10 Plus (3rd Gen), Galaxy Tab S7 FE 5G and Galaxy S24 FE in the comparison — and defer hardware gating until the PC algorithm works.** The available phone is an FE (Exynos 2400e); naming a standard S24 minimum would require its own identified SKU and measurements. On the budget tablet side, Story 10.18's R8-minified device gate D3 measured `corner_keypoints.tflite` at **6.35–6.46 ms median** on the Lenovo TB128XU (more than 2× faster than its ~14 ms debug timing), confirming lightweight LiteRT inference has real headroom even on the lowest tier — though a wider free-angle continuous workload still needs direct measurement. [5]

**Size budget (NFR-11, 30 MB installed ceiling) strongly favors staying on LiteRT.** Story 10.18's historical camera-enabled projection was **26.86 MB** (on a 12.41 MB release baseline), but by Story 10.23 `checkReleaseSize` had already grown to **12.98 MB (+575 KB)**, and `CameraSizePolicy.kt` charges `3 × classBytes` for post-10.18 camera code. Realistic current headroom is roughly **~2.0–2.5 MB** (or ~2.4–2.9 MB replacing the 406 KB corner model). An uncompressed 2.42 MB NanoDet FP32 pair leaves virtually no room for any added runtime, and a stock ncnn Android demo ships a 12.62 MB arm64 native library (including OpenCV/Vulkan). Measure `./gradlew :app:checkCameraSize` on the live branch before adopting any new runtime; a **0.2–0.8 MB LiteRT model** exported into the already-bundled `litert:2.3.2` runtime avoids native-library bloat completely. [2] [5] [7]

Only after the PC spike passes its exit bar should we replay identical recordings on each physical device to isolate compute cost, then run matched live captures to measure time to first correct placement, wrong accepted cells, correct reappearance, exact final map, manual corrections, and sustained camera-to-map delay. If only the phone delivers a usable scanner, propose a **camera-feature minimum** as a separate decision from whole-app support. The [tracker](tracker.md) holds the PC-first gates and device matrix.
