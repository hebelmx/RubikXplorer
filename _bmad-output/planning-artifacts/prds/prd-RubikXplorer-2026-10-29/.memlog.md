- (decision by party panel (delegated by PO)) Quick Check verified rule (party panel 2026-11-17, delegated by PO hebelmx, during Story 10.6): a Quick Check (Partial observation) returns VERIFIED only for a match (coverage holds and the single consistent candidate is []). A Partial mismatch with one consistent candidate is at most LIKELY: Sync State moves to PHYSICAL_MISMATCH, the candidate stays the leading hypothesis, and a Guided 6-face Scan is offered. Only a Full observation verifies a mismatch. Reason: FR-10 / Quick Check NFR 0 false verified outranks the FR-26 example; out-of-model errors made ~0.14% of Partial VERIFIED mismatches false; with the rule, 0 false VERIFIED and 99.8% match confirmation over the random suite.
