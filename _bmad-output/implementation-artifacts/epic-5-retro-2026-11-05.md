## Findings

### Spec-to-implementation reconciliation

1. **On the free cube, gestures and Move keys do not go through `MoveCommitted`.**
   - The 5.2 AC (`epics.md:1116-1118`) says a recognized gesture "goes through the same `MoveCommitted` path as a Move key".
   - The free cube has no Learning Session, so both inputs share `FreeCubeViewModel.onMoveIntent` → `MoveHistory.commit`. The gate flagged it as high (ledger 751, `FreeCubeViewModel.kt:54`).
   - The PO accepted the shared path as equivalent and asked for a follow-up story, a free-cube event/reducer owner, through `bmad-correct-course` after Epic 5 (ledger 754, 772; `spec-5-2-…md`, "Product owner decisions (a)"). Lesson gestures do commit through `SessionEvent` since 5.4 (`spec-5-4-…md`, Summary).
   - Disposition: **fix now (planning)** → action item 1.
2. **Show me's scope changed at the gate, against the spec, twice.**
   - 5.4: the spec fixed Show me with no view rotation "by design" (`spec-5-4-…md`, Residual risks). Gate round 4 failed on exactly that: target hidden in lesson-02-05 (ledger 817). The PO then approved shared ViewControls in the Demonstration player (ledger 818, `4da9676`).
   - 5.5: the intent's Never list excluded changing Show me's `DemonstrationPlayer`, and the follow-up review reverted the first pass's Show me summary for that reason (`182455e`). Gate round 1 failed because Show me lacked the summary (ledger 850). The fix put it back (`25426c0`).
   - Disposition: **accept** the as-built (PO-approved). Process lesson → action item 7.
3. **The 5.1 250 ms AC is measured only on the mid-range device.** The ledger records an R turn of about 214 ms over 14 frames on SM-S721U1, a Galaxy S24 FE (ledger 733). The minimum reference device (Tab S7 FE) is unmeasured and deferred to Story 1.11 (`spec-5-1-…md` deferred). Disposition: **defer** → action item 2.

### Cross-story boundaries (inline code lens)

4. **Double-tap reset ignores tap distance (5.3, still open).**
   - The gate passed with this medium open (ledger 780, 789).
   - `Cube3DRenderer.kt:458-459` compares only `uptimeMillis` with `doubleTapTimeoutMillis`, so two quick taps on opposite sides of the stage reset the view.
   - The 5.3 spec records `deferred: []`, and the finding reached no tracker.
   - Disposition: **fix now** → action item 3.
5. **The demonstration path carried most of the epic's gate failures.**
   - Highs in `DemonstrationPlayer.kt` and the Lesson demonstration wiring: 5.4 rounds 1, 2 and 4 (ledger 801, 806, 817) and 5.5 rounds 1 and 2 (ledger 850, 855). That is 5 of the epic's 13 gate highs.
   - Each one is a different consumer that reads "the shown Move" or "the shown cube" from a different source: the transport position, the FIFO queue, or `session.virtual`. 5.4 round 2 names this directly: "emphasis from transport position, arrow from queue".
   - The fixes converged on "drive from the presented Move" (`7eb10e0`, `fa4890b`, `5a00dca`) and "summarize the demonstration cube" (`1e98854`). Nothing stops a later consumer from reading the wrong source again.
   - Disposition: **defer** → folded into action item 4.
