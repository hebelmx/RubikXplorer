### Cross-story boundaries (the defects no single story saw)

1. **Stage 6 completion is recorded before the Learner confirms the solve (8.6 vs 6.x/7.x progress).** `SessionHost.dispatch` writes `LessonCompleted` and Stage progress as soon as `objectiveMet` flips, with no `syncState` check (`app/.../session/SessionHost.kt:474-483`). In physical mode `objectiveMet` is a prediction (Done taps or `EditorEntered`). 8.6 gated only the milestone overlay, with UI state: `physicalFirstSolveConfirmed` and `physicalSolveUnconfirmed` are `rememberSaveable` in `LessonScreen.kt:166-169`, and `pendingPhysicalSolve` (`LessonScreen.kt:228-234`) works around the early write. Consequences: the You tab shows Stage 6 complete even after *I did something else*; the unconfirmed flag is lost on close and reopen, so the mode-switch bypass that 8.6's review fixed returns after navigation. No test drives a `SessionHost` with a progress writer (all `PhysicalLessonScreenTest` hosts are `SessionHost(TickingClock())`). Lenses: adversarial 8, 9; edge-case; verification-gap 7, 8. Disposition: **fix now** → action item 1.
2. **The physical undo instruction never reaches the screen (8.1 vs 8.2).** 8.1 AC 2 requires the presentation to say "Turn R' on your cube to undo." The reducer emits `Presentation.Instruct` with `msg.physical.undo`, but `PresentationMapping.kt:11` maps `Instruct` to a plain `Animate` and drops the text; nothing else in `app/src/main` renders it. Physical **Redo** is worse: `Reduce.kt:129-136` turns it into a confirmed move with only an `Animate`, and the Redo button stays enabled in physical mode (`LessonScreen.kt:1029`). Both silently move `expected` away from the real cube. Lenses: adversarial 12, 13; edge-case. Disposition: **fix now** → action item 2.
3. **Switching to physical mode does not persist a physical Checkpoint (8.4 vs 6.2/6.5).** `switchMode` leaves `checkpoint` unchanged (`Reduce.kt:459-503`), so `persistCheckpoint` returns early (`Reduce.kt:931-932`). The 8.4 review removed `checkpoint = nextHistory` on purpose (`spec-8-4-...md:130`), but no test covers the consequence: until the next Objective boundary the stored Checkpoint says `VIRTUAL`, so `SessionHost.endSolve` or a checkpoint resume lands in virtual mode with no Confirm-your-cube card. The snapshot path (process kill) does carry `mode` and is not affected. Lenses: all three. Disposition: **fix now** → action item 3.
4. **Mismatch ladder gathers evidence it then throws away (8.3, 8.1 contract).**
   - Rung 1: in `PHYSICAL_MISMATCH`, an entered move only sets `lastDiagnosis` (`Reduce.kt:548-563`). `TRANSITIONS.md:251` says it "diagnoses the physical move evidence and commits it". There is no way out of `PHYSICAL_MISMATCH` except Undo, the editor or a mode switch (no dismiss for a false alarm).
   - The rung-1 base state changes meaning with hidden history: `history.undo().state` when `canUndo`, otherwise `expected` (`Reduce.kt:552-556`).
   - Rung 2: every corner answer just sets `rung = 3` (`MismatchLadder.kt:111-143`); `PhysicalObservation.Partial` is never produced.
   - Guided recovery offered after `EditorEntered` cannot be followed on a real cube: the first physical confirm goes through `withHistory`, which resets `guidedRecovery` to `None` (`Reduce.kt:533`, `:863`).
   - `PHYSICAL_VERIFIED` is never assigned by any transition (`SyncState.kt:10`; only read at `Reduce.kt:519`, `:576`).

   The 8.3 ACs are met at their letter (questions in order, move used as evidence, recovery offered), but the ladder does not resolve a mismatch. Lenses: adversarial 5, 6, 16, 17; edge-case; verification-gap 10, 11. Disposition: **fix now** (contract decision plus code) → action item 4.
5. **Physical Solution playback is not reachable in production, and has two latent bugs (8.5 vs 3.6/3.7).** `rememberSolutionPlaybackState` and `SolutionPlayback(` have no caller in `app/src/main`; only `SolutionPlaybackTest` uses them.
