## Findings

### Spec-to-implementation reconciliation

1. **Production wiring of solve suspension and resumption deferred to Story 3.7 (6.1 and 6.5).**
   - Story 6.1 and 6.5 introduced `SolvePlaybackStarted` and `SolvePlaybackEnded` (`SessionHost.endSolve`) to suspend active Lessons and resume them from Checkpoints.
   - Because the Solve tab and solver UI are built in Epic 3 (specifically Story 3.7), `endSolve` has no in-app production UI caller yet.
   - Disposition: **accept** as an approved medium deferral to Story 3.7.

2. **Instrumented 3D sync suite (`Cube3DRenderSyncTest`) deferred to device sessions (6.6).**
   - Story 6.6 refactored the free-cube state into a pure `reduceFreeCube` reducer in `:core:application` and refactored `FreeCubeViewModel` to be a pure event dispatcher.
   - `Cube3DRenderSyncTest` is an instrumented Android test in `app/src/androidTest` that requires a physical device or emulator. The 2D randomized sync suite (`SessionRenderSyncTest`) and screen gesture tests ran and passed on the JVM.
   - Disposition: **defer** execution of `Cube3DRenderSyncTest` to the scheduled hardware validation session alongside Epic 5 deferrals.

3. **PreferenceStore error recovery on disk failures (6.4).**
   - Story 6.4's gate noted two medium findings: save/reset write failures are logged but not exposed to the UI, and if initial load fails, a subsequent save could overwrite persisted preferences with defaults.
   - Disposition: **address in hardening (Epic 12)** → action item 1.

### Cross-story boundaries & architectural invariants

4. **Single-writer and Room persistence boundaries strictly maintained (AD-3, AD-10, AD-20).**
   - All session persistence flows through `SerialWriter` to ensure sequential database operations without blocking the main thread or violating hexagonal boundaries.
   - `:core:*` JVM modules remain 100% free of Android, Room, or UI imports.
   - Free cube remains strictly in-memory and outside `SessionHost` (AD-20).

5. **CI and test flakiness under high concurrency.**
   - Flaky test runs occurred 3 times across the epic during CI execution due to timing-sensitive Compose test rules (`LessonScreenTest`).
   - Disposition: **investigate timing/clock synchronization in Compose tests** → action item 2.
