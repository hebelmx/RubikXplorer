## Intent

**Problem:** Quick Scan's new continuous facelet scanning algorithm exists only as a Python PC spike (`pc_spike/lattice_fit.py`, `pc_spike/mapper.py` from PR #363) depending on OpenCV/NumPy, so the Android app has no deterministic, pure-JVM reader of record in `:core:domain` for geometry stitching, self-calibrated color anchoring, cubie invariants, twist detection, or downstream handoff to Review, Guided Scan, and the Manual Editor.

**Approach:** Port `FaceLatticeFitter` and `ContinuousFaceletMapper` (with pure-Kotlin projective DLT homography, shading-invariant CIELAB, WHITE-referenced hue distance, exact 8-per-color balanced solver, 24-SO(3) regrip merger, local cubie invariants, and mid-scan twist detection) into `:core:domain` (`dev.cubentia.domain.recognition.continuous`), wire canonical `Orientation.HOME` export and a typed domain handoff payload into `ScanAssembler` and `ScanReview`, commit a deterministic `Fixtures/corner/` LiteRT v2 INT8 peak parity fixture, and port the 7 end-to-end stress scenarios as JVM parity tests.

Refs (binding): FR-22, FR-23, FR-24 · NFR-4, NFR-12 · AD-1, AD-2, AD-9, AD-12, AD-14 · PR #363 (`pc_spike/lattice_fit.py`, `pc_spike/mapper.py`) · Stories 10.1, 10.3, 10.16, 10.17, #321, #323.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:**
- Pure JVM Kotlin in `:core:domain` (`dev.cubentia.domain.recognition.continuous`); zero Android, Compose, CameraX, LiteRT, or OpenCV imports (`checkModuleBoundaries` passes).
- Neutral per-frame inputs only (2D points/quads, 9 sampled sRGB patches, quality/sharpness/glare scalars); never raw image buffers or pixel arrays.
- Completed 54-sticker states canonicalize to `Orientation.HOME` (`WHITE` on U, `GREEN` on F, `Facelets` URFDLB row-major) and validate via `CubeValidator`; never guess or fill missing/uncertain cells from expected state.
- Sealed domain snapshots and cue types with `Text("msg.quick-scan.continuous.*", ...)` keys in `en.json` (`checkLearnerText` + `ShippedContentTest` pass; no multi-word prose literals in `src/main/kotlin` outside allowed exception/check calls; hyphenated keys only).
- Preserve all existing public APIs and behavior of `ScanAssembler`, `ScanReview`, `SuspectRanking`, `StickerClassifier`, and Guided Scan (additive overloads/methods only).
- `:core:domain` Kover line coverage stays `≥ 80%` (`dev.cubentia.domain.cube` `≥ 90%`).

**Never:**
- No Android camera code, LiteRT model assets, `build-logic` flag changes, or shadow-reader removal (Story 10.31).
- No Compose UI, `ContinuousQuickScanScreen`, or 2-corner Quick Scan deletion (Story 10.32).
- No modification to `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/`, `_bmad/`, or `.agents/skills/`.
- No modification to `StickerClassifier.deltaE` (CIE76 stays untouched for Guided Scan).
- No requirement on saved FR-24 `ColorCalibration` for continuous scans (always optional).

## Design Notes

- **Raw-quad single sampling vs alignment roll:** In `mapper.py`, `_sample_face_cells` warps an aligned quad (`roll(rawQuad, -shift)`) to `96×96` and computes symmetric per-cell statistics (`idx == 4` 4-corner outer ring, `idx != 4` inner 48% square, rotation-invariant `q_angle` and `q_sharp`). Because rolling vertices by `-shift` is a `shift × 90°` CCW rotation of the 3×3 grid (`new[r, c] = old[c, 2 - r]` repeated `shift` times, keeping `idx == 4` invariant), `QuadObservation` accepts raw CW quad corners + `cellRgbs: List<RgbColor>` (9 sRGB `[0..255]` patches) + `cellQualities: List<Double>` (9 pre-multiplied cell quality weights in `(0.0, 1.0]`) + `sharpness: Double` + `hasGlare: Boolean`, and `ContinuousFaceletMapper` rotates `cellRgbs` and `cellQualities` by `shift` CCW whenever it aligns a quad by `roll(corners, -shift)`.
- **DLT homography solver (pure JVM):** Normalize `src` and `dst` points to zero mean and mean distance `sqrt(2)` (Hartley normalization), build the `2n × 9` design matrix `A` (or accumulate symmetric `9×9` `M = A^T A` directly), run cyclic Jacobi eigendecomposition (converges in `< 10` sweeps for `9×9`) to extract the eigenvector of the smallest eigenvalue, reshape to `3×3`, denormalize `H = T_dst^-1 * H_norm * T_src`, and normalize `H[2][2] = 1` (rejecting degenerate `|H[2][2]| < 1e-12` or near-singular `|det(H)| < 1e-12`). Exact for 4 points and least-squares optimal for `6..9` points, matching `cv2.findHomography(..., method=0)` to `< 1e-6` px.
- **Canonicalization & partial handoff frame:** Inside `ContinuousFaceletMapper`, `master` uses local fragment face names (`F, R, B, L, U, D`) until `globalR` maps them into `Orientation.HOME` (`U=WHITE, F=GREEN, R=RED, D=YELLOW, L=ORANGE, B=BLUE`). When `globalR` is known (all completed scans and anchored partial scans with `≥ 3` centers), every confirmed face exports in canonical `Orientation.HOME` URFDLB order with `orientationLocked = true` (adjacent-edge mutual orientation already resolved) and its 9 self-calibrated/AWB-adjusted `Lab`s + `StickerConfidence`. When `globalR` is not yet known (e.g. 1–2 confirmed faces before global anchoring), confirmed faces export with their local mutual adjacency/colors + `orientationLocked = false` (or `emptyMap()` for URFDLB fallback if unanchored), and `ScanAssembler.assembleHandover` preserves kept face colors without reclassifying while rotating only unanchored faces / Guided `FaceSample`s.
- **CheckLearnerText compliance:** In `src/main/kotlin`, never place multi-word string literals in normal functions/constants; use `Text("msg.quick-scan.continuous.<hyphenated-key>", args)` for all cues and single-token/hyphenated identifiers elsewhere.
