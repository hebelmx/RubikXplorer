## Unanswered questions

1. **Completion hand-off (auto-advance).** The mapper emits the "All 54 stickers verified." cue on the same frame it reports the scan complete, and the story says Quick Scan opens Scan Review "when the mapper completes". If Review opens at once, the Learner never sees that cue and TalkBack has no screen to announce completion on. Which is it: (a) open Scan Review immediately, and announce completion on the Review screen only; (b) [Recommended] show the completed net and the cue for a short fixed time (about 1 s, analysis stopped, controls still working), announce it, then open Review automatically; or (c) wait for a tap on a *Review* button?
2. **Reset.** The story says *Reset* "clears the mapper state in place" and names no confirmation, but EXPERIENCE.md:219 says rare or destructive scan controls "each ask for a confirm". (i) Does *Reset* ask before clearing when at least 1 side is mapped — (a) [Recommended] no, it clears at once (the scan restarts in seconds), or (b) yes, with a confirm dialog (title and button copy needed)? (ii) Does *Reset* leave the torch as the Learner set it [Recommended], or turn it off?
3. **Layer turned mid-scan (clearing).** The mapper latches the twist: every later frame returns "A layer turned during the scan…" with no tracked faces until it is reset. What does the screen do: (a) [Recommended] keep the net as it was, show the cue, and wait for the Learner to tap *Reset* (drawn as the emphasised action while the twist is latched); or (b) clear the map automatically after showing the cue (for how long)? And while the twist is latched, may *Scan face by face* and *Enter colors instead* still carry the faces mapped before the twist [Recommended: yes, the mapper's export keeps only the faces confirmed before the twist], or must they start empty?
4. **Net and outline visuals (animation, state-dependent drawing).** Planning says only "outlines tracked faces", "smoothed" quads and a "compact growing net". Please confirm or change these defaults: (i) stickers appear in the net with no animation the moment they are committed; (ii) a sticker not yet read is drawn as the same blank dark well the Scan Review uses, without the "?" glyph; (iii) conflicted stickers get the shared `sticker-highlight` outline (never red), on the net only; (iv) face outlines on the preview are a single `sticker-highlight` stroke per tracked face with no cell grid, eased toward the newest detection each frame (exponential smoothing, about half the distance per frame) and removed when the face has not been tracked for 3 frames; (v) no haptics, sound or first-use hint.
5. **"Scan face by face" edge cases.** (i) Before the map is anchored (fewer than 2 adjoining faces placed), the domain hand-off drops every face, so the Guided Scan would ask for all six even if the Learner saw stickers appear. Is that acceptable, with "Sides mapped: 0/6" shown until anchoring [Recommended], or should the action be disabled until a face can be kept? (ii) With 6/6 sides mapped but a conflict still open, the Guided Scan has no missing face to ask for. Should *Scan face by face* then (a) [Recommended] ask the Guided Scan to retake only the face(s) holding conflicted stickers, (b) open Scan Review directly with the conflicted stickers blank, or (c) be disabled?
6. **TalkBack announcements.** "Announces side-count milestones, cues, conflict face, and completion" does not say which counts or how often. Confirm: one polite live region for the cue line, re-announced only when the cue text changes; "Sides mapped: N/6" announced politely each time N increases (1 to 6) and not when it falls; no assertive announcements. Or name the milestones you want (for example 3/6 and 6/6 only).
7. **2-corner removal scope (the story names files that are still in use).** The story lists `CornerHold.kt` and `SideLight.kt` for removal, but `CornerHold` is used by Case capture (`CaseCaptureController`, `CASE_RECOGNITION`) and `SideLight` by the fixture recorder and the recorded fixture schema; `QuickScanPlan` (in `QuickScan.kt`) is used by the recorder, `LiveFixture` and two device tests; `QuickScanClassification` is used by the Story 10.23 `exportKotlinStickerReads` evaluation tool. Choose: (a) [Recommended] delete `QuickScan.kt`, `QuickScanClassification.kt` and `QuickScanDiagnosis.kt` (moving `QuickScanPlan` and `CenterCheck` to a neutral file), keep `CornerHold.kt` and `SideLight.kt` trimmed to what Case capture and the recorder use, and delete the 10.23 `exportKotlinStickerReads` and `evaluateQuickScan` tools with their tests; or (b) also remove the recorder's light check so `SideLight.kt` can go (this changes the recorded fixture schema); or (c) keep the 10.23 evaluation tool by keeping `QuickScanClassification.kt`.

## Settled without a question (will go into the spec as written)

- **Cue line:** the screen renders the one cue the mapper emits (`ContinuousScanCue.toText()`, ten `msg.quick-scan.continuous.*` keys already in `en.json`); the shipped wording stands, and cue priority is the mapper's (`ContinuousFaceletMapper.computeCue`).
- **Torch:** OFF by default, shown only with a flash unit, turned off on hand-over, leave and background, and it stays off on return until the Learner turns it on again.
- **Close / Back:** leaves at once at 0 mapped sides; otherwise the existing "Discard this scan?" dialog (`msg.scan.discard-title`, `btn.scan.discard`, `btn.scan.cancel`).
- **Enter colors instead:** no discard question; at 1 or more mapped sides it seeds the Editor from `ContinuousScanHandoff.toFallbackStickers()`, at 0 it opens the blank Editor with `msg.solve.scan-fell-back`.
- **BETA label:** stays on the Solve tab's Quick Scan button only (EXPERIENCE.md:145), none inside the scan screen.
- **Rotation:** no orientation lock; the map survives a rotation.
